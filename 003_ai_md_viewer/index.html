Frequently Asked Questions

Is my document uploaded to an external server or cloud?

No. AI Markdown Reader executes entirely within your browser client. Your documents, code snippets, and confidential design specs never leave your computer.

Can I use this offline without an active internet connection?

Yes. Once loaded in your browser tab, all parsing, diagram rendering, and formatting execute 100% locally in your browser memory without needing ongoing network connectivity.

Why is this viewer completely silent?

In quiet corporate offices, libraries, and open workspaces, unexpected sound effects can be disruptive. This application is purposefully built with zero audio triggers, featuring smooth visual confetti and sleek micro-animations instead.
