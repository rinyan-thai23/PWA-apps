Key Features for Road & Indoor Cyclists

Rhythm Audio by Ear

Internalize cadence rhythms through your earphones without looking at a screen. Includes 8 distinct synthesizer tones and stereo pedal cues.

Lock Screen Remote

Full MediaSession API support. Adjust cadence by ±5 RPM and pause/resume directly from your mobile lock screen or Bluetooth earbud buttons.

Fullscreen Indoor HUD

Designed for tablets or external monitors during roller / turbo trainer workouts. Features dynamic 4-color cadence zones and keyboard hotkeys.

Gear Ratio & Speed

Calculate virtual speed based on chainring (50T/34T), rear cassette sprocket (11-34T), and tire circumference. Shift gears virtually in real-time.

Interval Simulator

Custom structured interval routines with automatic rhythm transitions, 3-second countdown audio beeps, and synthetic speech voice cues.

100% Offline PWA

Zero cloud servers, zero battery-draining telemetry. Installable directly to your home screen on iOS and Android with full offline capability.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep the metronome playing when I lock my phone?

Simply tap the START button and lock your screen. Cadence 90 runs a silent background audio loop coupled with the MediaSession API, allowing Web Audio to play continuously through your earphones in your jersey pocket.

Why train for a 90 RPM cadence?

90 RPM is the global benchmark for efficient endurance cycling. Lower cadences (<70 RPM) place heavy isometric stress on knee joints and slow-twitch muscle fibers, causing premature fatigue. A higher, fluid cadence (85–95 RPM) utilizes your aerobic cardiovascular engine for sustainable speed.

How do I install the app on my phone?

On iPhone Safari, tap the Share button and select "Add to Home Screen". On Android Chrome, tap the three dots menu and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen".
